Ideas from Gemmini on how to train 
```
Phase 1: Macro Pre-Training (The "Grammar" Phase)

    Datasets: MAESTRO (MIDI-only) + PDMX (Filtered for valid MIDI/MusicXML).

    Objective: Teach the model western music theory, chord progressions, rhythm, and structural phrasing.

    How to execute:

        Parse MAESTRO and extract only the highest melodic voice (the top note of chords) to prevent the model from learning multi-note piano clusters.

        Filter PDMX using its CSV metadata to grab monophonic or single-track melodic scores.

        Train your LSTM or Transformer using standard next-token cross-entropy loss over a sliding window.

        Result: A model that understands how musical sentences and scales work conceptually.

Phase 2: Domain Adaptation (The "Violin Physics" Phase)

    Dataset: Zenodo Violin MIDI Dataset.

    Objective: Adapt the model to the physical constraints, finger ranges, bow changes, and stylistic phrasing unique to the violin.

    How to execute:

        Freeze or lightly weight the early foundational layers of your model from Phase 1.

        Continue training exclusively on the Zenodo Violin etudes and performances.

        Because these files capture actual violin performances (including micro-timing and phrasing shifts), the network learns what sequences are physically playable on a fingerboard versus what is just theoretically possible on a piano.

        Result: A model that speaks fluent "violin."

Phase 3: Style Fine-Tuning (The "Personal ID" Phase)

    Dataset: Your own recorded and transcribed audio sessions (converted to MIDI via basic-pitch or librosa).

    Objective: Specialize the model entirely on your personal tendencies, technical strengths, and improvisational habits.

    How to execute:

        Take your personal dataset and split it into training and validation sets.

        Run a final, low-learning-rate training loop over your model. Because the network already knows music theory (Phase 1) and violin mechanics (Phase 2), it will quickly adapt to your specific stylistic fingerprint without overfitting or breaking.

        Result: A next-note predictor tailored to anticipate your exact playing habits.
```
